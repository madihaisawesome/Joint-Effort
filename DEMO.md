# Demo script (judging)

**Pitch (10s):** JointEffort is a privacy-first movement coach — your camera stays on your device, Gemini gives natural voice cues and tips, and you can also find community activity around campus.

## Setup checklist

- [ ] `backend` running on :3000 with `GEMINI_API_KEY`
- [ ] `JointEffort` Vite on :5173
- [ ] `/api/health` shows gemini + tts configured
- [ ] Good lighting; torso in frame (closer is OK)

## Live path

1. **Home** — show brand + accessibility feel (type size / contrast if asked)
2. **Exercises → Judge Demo** — short 2-rep routine
3. Stick demo speaks instructions → **I’m ready**
4. Do 1–2 arm raises — hear “too high/low” and “Good. X more.”
5. Advance — next step instructions speak
6. Optional sit-to-stand or skip to finish
7. **Activity** or **Events** — community layer (30s)

## Talking points

- Video never leaves the device
- Gemini for TTS + coaching text only
- Built for calm, accessible guidance — not a clinic product
